## In-person summit

The first ManyPrimates in-person meeting is scheduled to take place on December 7th, 2026, at the St Andrews Botanic Gardens in Scotland.

Our plan is to bring people together to discuss the state of our ongoing projects, and also actively discuss and brainstorm on the future of ManyPrimates, including sustainability, inclusivity, diversity, and connectivity across our network, as well as future projects including MP4!

The summit is currently closed to in-person registration, but the meeting will be as accessible as we can make it, and you are still very welcome to attend remotely. Please stay tuned for future details via the main email list, and get in touch with the coordinators if you have any queries.

## Ongoing project meetings

Active ManyPrimates projects regularly meet online to organize and carryout our research. Current active projects include ManyPrimates 2: delay of gratification, ManyPrimates 3: inference by exclusion, and ManyManys 1: reversal learning. If you are interested in getting involved with any of these projects and/or contributing to meetings, please contact the coordinators at manyprimates\[@]gmail-dot\*com in the first instance.
